# Medical Radiations Research Group — RMIT University

We develop AI and machine learning methods for medical imaging, adaptive
radiotherapy, nuclear medicine, and medical physics. Our work is grounded in
clinical problems and aimed at translational impact.

We are part of the
[Accelerator for Translational Research and Clinical Trials (ATRACT)](https://www.rmit.edu.au/about/schools-colleges/stem-college/research/leading-research-centres/atract-centre)
at RMIT University, Melbourne, Australia.
